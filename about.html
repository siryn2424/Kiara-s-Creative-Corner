Maricar "Karen" Decipeda — Founder & Maker

"Hi, I'm Karen! I am a proud mother of three, and I named Kiara's Creative Corner after my youngest child. Being a mom is my biggest motivation to keep going, keep trying, and build something meaningful while doing what I truly love. Every handmade crochet piece, playful keychain, and comforting candle I create is made with patience, warmth, and the same care I put into everything for my family."

The Story of Kiara's Creative Corner

What began back in 1998, when Karen was in college and crocheted a lanyard phone case just as mobile phones started trending, quickly turned into a campus favorite that everyone wanted to carry. That love for making things by hand never left her, eventually growing into a collection of handmade bouquets, everyday accessories, playful keychains, and comforting candles. Every piece is crafted patiently so it carries genuine warmth from her hands to yours, making each item feel special, whether it is a small gift or a personal treat.

Goals & Advocacy

Machines can copy many things, but they can never crochet a single stitch. Our goal is to celebrate the beauty of slow, unhurried craftsmanship in a fast paced world. Through every handmade piece we create, we hope to inspire appreciation for the time, patience, and heart that make artisan work truly one of a kind.
